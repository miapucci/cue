# Cue — Design System

Visual identity and UX rules for the iOS app. Dark-first, dopamine-reserved accents, one primary action per screen.

---

## Brand

- **Name:** Cue — “Your cue to create.”
- **App Store / hero:** “The fastest way to cast and get cast.”
- **Creators:** “Find who you need. Get the video.”
- **Talent:** “Get paid to be on camera.”

**Voice:** Cool friend who knows the industry. Direct, confident, a little playful. Never corporate.

| Do | Don’t |
|----|--------|
| “Your scene. Your terms.” | “Connecting talent with opportunity” |
| “Drop a brief. Get a banger.” | “Post your casting requirements” |
| “Who’s filming your next one?” | “Find qualified performers” |
| Short sentences. White space. | Paragraphs. Bullet lists of features. |

**References:** Notion (calm confidence) × Depop (creator-native) × Cash App (money feels cool).

---

## Color (Electric Violet Replaced)

**Background**

- **Surface:** Near-black `#0D0D0D` — default experience, premium.

**Primary accent (choose one)**

Electric violet is replaced by one of:

| Option        | Hex     | Use when |
|---------------|---------|----------|
| **Electric Blue** ⭐ | `#00D4FF` | Default. Camera lens, digital, tech-forward. |
| Warm Orange   | `#FF6B35` | “Lights, camera, action” — heat, hustle. |
| Pure White    | `#F5F5F5` | Minimal; let lime and pink carry color. |

Use the chosen primary for: key UI (tabs, links, selected states), brand moments, creativity/performance.

**Reserved accents (dopamine moments only)**

- **Success / money:** Lime green `#C8F135` — earnings confirmed, video approved, payment released.
- **CTA / urgency:** Cyber pink `#F72585` — main buttons, high-impact actions.

Rule: Neon (green/pink) only on reward and conversion moments. Overuse weakens the effect.

---

## Typography

- **Headers:** Clash Display (or Poppins) — bold, editorial.
- **Body:** Inter — medium weight, readable.
- **Micro-copy:** TikTok-style caption energy; how Gen Z talks.

---

## App Icon

- Letter **Q** (for Cue) as clapperboard/camera shutter, or bold **Q** with one neon accent on black.
- Must read at small sizes (home screen).

---

## Motion

- **Reward moments only:** Lime green pulse on earnings; confetti on video approved; satisfying green check, not clinical.
- **Micro-interactions:** Every “done” or “success” moment feels alive. Don’t animate everything.

---

## UX Rules

1. **Dark mode default** — primary experience, not a toggle.
2. **One primary action per screen** — 8-second rule; no dual CTAs.
3. **Brief browsing = swipe feed** — cards with vibe preview, pay rate, deadline. Tap for details. Variable reward, not search-first.
4. **Cards:** Aesthetic reference + pay rate + deadline. Nothing else on the card face.
