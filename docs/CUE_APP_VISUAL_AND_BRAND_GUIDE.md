# Cue App Visual & Brand Guide (for Manus agents)

**Copy this into Manus** when you create agents or start tasks, so agents know what the app looks like and can generate relevant, on-brand content and image prompts.

---

## What Cue is (one sentence)

Cue is an app where **creators** post video briefs and **talent** get cast and get paid. Two sides: "I need video" (creator) and "I want to get cast" (talent).

---

## Tagline and copy (use exactly)

- **Tagline:** Your cue to create.
- **Subline:** Find talent or get cast. Pick your side.
- **Creator card:** "I need video" — Find talent. Get the video.
- **Talent card:** "I want to get cast" — Get paid to be on camera.

(Source: app onboarding, Role Select screen.)

---

## App look and feel

- **UI:** Dark mode only. Background near black (#0D0D0D). Cards and inputs slightly lighter (elevated surface) for depth.
- **Primary accent:** Electric blue (#00D4FF) — links, key UI elements.
- **Success / money:** Lime green (#C8F135) — earnings, approval, payment released.
- **High-impact CTAs:** Cyber pink (#F72585) — primary buttons, urgency.
- **Text:** White primary; white at 70% opacity for secondary. Serif for logo and hero; clean, premium, minimal clutter.
- **Tone in product:** Confident, clear, a bit bold. No jargon. Benefit-led.

---

## Key screens (describe to agents; attach screenshots if possible)

1. **Role Select (first screen):** "Cue" logo at top. Headline: "Your cue to create." Subline: "Find talent or get cast. Pick your side." Two large cards: (1) "I need video" / Find talent. Get the video. (2) "I want to get cast" / Get paid to be on camera. Dark background, accent on card edges and chevrons.
2. **Create account / Sign in:** After picking a role, user sees "Create account" and "Sign in" (email+password or Sign in with Apple).
3. **Creator:** Home with briefs; post a new brief; view submissions.
4. **Talent:** Feed of briefs to browse; tap to see details and submit video; earnings view.
5. **Brief card:** Title, description, pay, deadline, orientation/duration; clear CTA (Submit / View submissions).

When generating **image prompts** for social or ads, agents should reference this look: dark, premium, electric blue accent, app-style screens or mockups when relevant.
